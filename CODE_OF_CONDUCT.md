# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

Participants are expected to communicate respectfully, welcome constructive review, and protect the privacy and safety of everyone involved.

## Expected behavior

- Give specific, evidence-based, and actionable feedback.
- Respect scope, consent, privacy, and trust boundaries.
- Keep public examples fictional and safe to publish.
- Acknowledge uncertainty, limitations, and mistakes promptly.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or sustained personal attacks.
- Publishing private personal, account, repository, host, network, or task identifiers.
- Sharing credentials, access tokens, or non-public evidence.
- Pressuring others to bypass safety, review, or approval boundaries.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when behavior violates these expectations. Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers when possible; do not disclose sensitive details in a public report.
